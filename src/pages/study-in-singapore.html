Questions

FAQs

How do I apply?

Message us on WhatsApp with the course you are interested in. We explain the steps, the documents needed and the next intake.

Do I need IELTS?

It depends on the course and the institution. Tell us your course and we will confirm the English requirement.

How much do the courses cost?

Fees depend on the programme and are set by the institution. We share the current fees when you enquire.

Can you help with the student pass?

Yes. We guide you through the documents and steps. The Singapore government makes the final decision.

Can you book my flight too?

Yes. We offer special fares from Trichy, Chennai and Coimbatore to Singapore. See flight fares.

Can I work while I study?

Rules for student pass holders are set by the Singapore government and can change. Our team shares the current rules when you enquire.

Admissions, fees and student pass decisions are made by each institution and the Singapore authorities. Sirpy Air Travels helps with information and applications. Student names, quotes and images on this page are samples until real ones are added.
