Study in Singapore page: image slots
=====================================
Put your own photos in this folder with these exact names. They appear on the page
automatically; until then the page shows coloured sample placeholders.

hero.png         Hero student photo. Transparent PNG works best, about 900 x 1200 px.
student-1.jpg    Student posts (5). Landscape or square, about 800 x 700 px.
student-2.jpg
student-3.jpg
student-4.jpg
student-5.jpg

After adding images, open http://127.0.0.1:8123/study-in-singapore to check them.

Student names, institution and course are still sample text. Edit them in
src/pages/study-in-singapore.html (search for "Student 1"), then run:  npm run pages
